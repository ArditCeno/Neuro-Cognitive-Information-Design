Privacy Policy

Data controller

Ardit Ceno, independent researcher. Contact: arditceno82@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Registration: age, profession, expertise level, and an anonymous identifier. Providing your name is optional.
  • Eye-tracking: gaze coordinates (x, y, time) produced by the webcam.
  • Reading and task timings, and your answers to the comprehension and workload questionnaires.
  • Aggregate eye-blink rate and head orientation.

We do not record or store video, audio or images. The camera frames are processed in your browser only, to estimate gaze coordinates.

Purpose and legal basis

Data is used to study how document presentation affects readability and comprehension. Processing is based on your explicit consent, given before the session starts.

Storage and access

Data is stored in a database hosted in the European Union and is accessible only to the researcher. Data is linked to an anonymous identifier, not to your name or email.

Retention

Identifiable information is kept only as long as needed for the study. Aggregated, anonymous results may be retained for academic publication.

Your rights

You may withdraw at any time; the study includes a button to delete your session data. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing the contact above.

Local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to keep the session active and to remember your language choice. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

Age

The study is intended for participants aged 16 and over.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.
