Privacy Policy

Data controller

Ardit Ceno, independent researcher. Contact: arditceno82@gmail.com.

The study is run as independent research. There is no institutional ethics board behind it; participation is based on the informed consent you give before starting.

What we collect

  • Registration: age, profession, expertise level, and an anonymous identifier (for example USR_12345). Giving your name is optional.
  • Eye-tracking: gaze coordinates (x, y, time), blink samples and head orientation produced by the webcam. Raw gaze samples are stored so the analysis can be reproduced.
  • Reading and task timings, and your answers to the comprehension and workload questionnaires.
  • Aggregate eye-blink rate and head orientation.

We do not record or store video, audio or images. The camera frames are processed in your browser only, to estimate gaze coordinates.

How the data is kept apart

The research dataset contains the anonymous identifier and the measurements. If you provide your name, it is stored in a separate, access-restricted identity table in the study database and also appears in the emailed report. The name is never part of the analysis export.

Purpose and legal basis

Data is used to study how document presentation affects readability and comprehension. Processing is based on your explicit consent, given before the session starts (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

Storage, processors and access

Sessions are stored in a Cloudflare D1 (SQL) database, raw gaze is stored compressed in Cloudflare D1 (or Cloudflare R2 when enabled), and the report is emailed through Google Apps Script / Google Workspace. Cloudflare and Google act as data processors. Access is limited to the researcher; database exports are protected by a separate access token, and the identity table is kept apart from the analysis data.

Retention

Names and other identifiable information are kept only as long as needed for the study and for verifying participation, and are deleted on request. Anonymised measurements may be retained for academic publication. Email copies of reports are deleted when a deletion request is handled.

Your rights

You may withdraw at any time without giving a reason. To access, correct or delete your data, email the contact above with your participant ID (shown at the end of the session). Deletion requests are handled within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Local storage

The site uses your browser's local storage to keep the session active, to retry uploads and to remember your language choice. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

Age

The study is intended for participants aged 18 and over.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.
