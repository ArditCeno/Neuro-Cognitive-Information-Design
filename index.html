A/B Testing across 5 Professional Fields

Field Version A (Traditional) Version B (Optimised) Key research question
Law — Neuro-Legal 6-line sentences, Latin terms, no visual space Plain Language, clear headings Do plain-language contracts cut fixation duration and re-reading?
Finance — Neuro-Finance Dense P&L number table Data Storytelling chart + highlighted KPIs Are anomalies spotted faster in a chart than in a table?
Business — Neuro-Management Two-page long-text proposal Executive Summary with visual cards & bullets How much does information retention improve?
Health — Neuro-Health Unstructured clinical chart, non-standard abbreviations SOAP format or colour-coded Triage Dashboard Does visual design lower critical decision time in urgency?
Software — Neuro-Software Deeply nested if/else Guard Clauses, linear structure How much are eye movements (regressions) reduced?
