Biology - mid - M1 - Photosynthesis and respiration

  • Photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6O2
  • Cellular respiration: C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O + ATP
  • Photosynthesis stores energy; respiration releases it
  • Glycolysis occurs in the cytoplasm
  • Krebs cycle in the mitochondrial matrix
