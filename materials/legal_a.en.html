Service Agreement — Clauses 4, 7 and 9

Clause 4. Liability. The providing Party shall be liable to the receiving Party for direct damages arising from the breach of binding contractual obligations under this Agreement, provided that the aggregate liability of the providing Party, regardless of the legal basis or the nature of the claim, shall in no event exceed the total amount of fees paid by the receiving Party during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the damage.

Clause 7. Confidentiality shall remain in force for a period of five (5) years following the termination or expiry of this Agreement, irrespective of the grounds for termination, and shall oblige both parties to preserve the confidentiality of sensitive commercial information.

Clause 9. Notice and termination. The Party alleging a breach shall notify the other Party in writing within thirty (30) days of becoming aware of the breach. Either Party may terminate the Agreement with sixty (60) days' prior written notice. In the event of an irreparable breach, termination shall be immediate.

Each party shall bear its own legal costs. The governing law shall be the law of the Republic of Albania.
