Nursing - fillestar - M2 - Hand hygiene

  • WHO: 5 moments: before patient contact, before aseptic procedure, after body fluid exposure, after patient contact, after contact with the patient's surroundings
  • Soap and water: 40-60 seconds
  • Alcohol rub: 20-30 seconds
  • Hands must be dry after washing
  • When visibly soiled: soap and water
