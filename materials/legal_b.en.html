Service Agreement — Key Clauses at a Glance

Liability

The provider's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the last 12 months.

Confidentiality

Lasts 5 years after the contract ends.

Key deadlines

  • Breach notice: within 30 days.
  • Termination: with 60 days' prior notice.
  • Irreparable breach: immediate termination.

Costs and law

Each party bears its own legal costs. Governing law: Albania.
