## Export to PDF (Chrome or Safari)
1. Open the page (live link above, or the file).
2. **Ctrl/Cmd + P**.
3. Destination **Save as PDF**, Margins **None**, and tick **Background graphics**.
4. For the poster choose paper size **A1**; for the slides paper **16:9** (or "fit to page").
   For the abstract, **A4**.

## Word (.docx)
Open `abstract.html` in **Microsoft Word** (File → Open) and **Save As → .docx**.
Word keeps the headings and text; adjust fonts if you wish.

## What you add yourself (marked in red `[ ... ]`)
- **Logos:** your institution logo and the official conference logo
  (top-right slots in `poster.html` and `slides.html`; the dark header in `abstract.html`).
  The NCID logo (`../assets/logo.png`) is already placed.
- **Photos / screenshots:** the dashed box in the poster ("Add a photo…") and the slide notes.
- **Results:** the `[ ... ]` placeholders in the Results sections — replace with real data
  after collection. The chart values are illustrative; swap them for the study's numbers.
- **Affiliation:** `[Institution, Faculty, City]` in all three files.
